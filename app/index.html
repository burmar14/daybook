Daybook

A wall chart for your year, sized for your pocket.

  • Grid
    Twelve columns, one per monthThirty-one rows down the side. Every square is a single day.
  • Zoom
    Year, month, week, dayThe closer you go, the more you see at once — by Week view every category is on screen together.
  • Tap
    A tap logs the dayTap again to move up the scale. Tap past the top to clear it.
  • Hold
    Hold for the detailsExact numbers, a note to yourself, or clear the day.
  • Tabs
    Track what you wantSix to start. Turn any off, or add your own in Settings.
  • Safe
    It never leaves this phoneNo account, no upload. Export a backup any time.
For the real thing, tap Share then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app and works offline.
